Lightning

Every public node and channel in a snapshot of the XBT Lightning graph, taken by a node of the network and published here. Node size follows its number of channels; a link's length and thickness follow the channel's capacity (summed when two nodes share several). The map checks for new data every 2 minutes while it is open. Private channels are not in the public graph.

Shops

A shop sits on the Lightning node it is paid at: the node that signs for its link key, or the node its hashrate offer names. A shop on a private node is shown at the graph's edge. Its icons are what it sells: web apps and films, files, AI, hashrate, VPN exits.

Relays

A Stream relay carries encrypted traffic between a buyer's app and a shop when they can't connect directly, and can't read it. It moves traffic, not payments, so it is drawn beside the shops that use it, not in the channel graph. The Nostr relays where the listings are found are drawn along the map's bottom edge. Nobody's address, balance or payments are shown.

Lightning Stream

Peer-to-peer streaming and payments for services, on Lightning, on top of Bitcoin. Paid as you use them. No accounts, no custody.
