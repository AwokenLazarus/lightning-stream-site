Lightning

Every public node and channel in a snapshot of the XBT Lightning graph, taken by a node of the network and published here. Node size follows its number of channels. Private channels are not in the public graph.

Lightning Stream

Shops, hashrate offers and VPN exits from their signed Nostr listings, joined to the Stream relays they name, to the Nostr relays they were found on, and, for a hashrate offer, to the Lightning node it is paid at.

Not shown

Nobody's address, balance or payments. The graph is public gossip, and this site keeps only node keys, aliases, colours and channel capacities.

Lightning Stream

Buy and sell services over XBT Lightning, paid as you use them. No accounts, no custody.
