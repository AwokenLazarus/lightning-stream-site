Live · from Nostr

Shops and services, as listed now.

Sellers of services paid over Bitcoin (XBT) Lightning publish signed listings on Nostr relays. This page reads the relays the app reads by default, every few minutes, and checks each listing as the app does: the event's signature, the listing's signature, and the key that binds them.

LiveLoading the latest listings…

Unvetted. A signed listing proves which key published it, not who runs the shop or what it delivers. Prices are the sellers' own and can change: the app shows the current price before it pays, and never pays a shop more than its listing shows. This site lists what is published; it does not recommend any shop.

Buying from a shop

Add it in your app, by its key.

In Lightning Stream, open Buy → Sellers, paste the shop's key and press Add seller (or use the found on Nostr link at the bottom of Browse and press Add this shop), then press Allow this seller to be paid and sign.

Where this comes from

Shops: Nostr kind 30402 events tagged lightning-stream. Hashrate: kind 30402 tagged lightning-stream-hashrate. VPN exits and Stream relays: kind 30078 tagged lightning-stream-vpn-exit and lightning-stream-relay. Read from the app's four default relays. Addresses a listing carries for direct connections are never shown here.

Raw JSON: /api/listings

Lightning Stream

Peer-to-peer streaming and payments for services, on Lightning, on top of Bitcoin. Paid as you use them. No accounts, no custody.
